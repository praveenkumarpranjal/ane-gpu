Model correctly applies chat template and stops at EOS token.

## Timing Breakdown
- Weight load: ~450-600 ms (mmap + Metal buffer creation)
- Engine setup: ~14 ms (MPS objects, RoPE tables, KV cache)
- Prefill: ~32-48 ms/token (sequential, no batching)
- Decode: ~41-49 ms/token (depends on cache length)

## Architecture
- fp16 weights (BF16→FP16 converted from safetensors)
- fp16 activations throughout
- fp32 accumulators in RMSNorm, attention softmax
- MPS fp16 matmuls for projections (Q, K, V, O, gate, up, down, lm_head)
- Metal compute for: embedding, RMSNorm, RoPE, bias add, silu_mul, residual
- Fused online-softmax GQA decode attention (no N² score matrix)
- Per-layer KV cache [128, 2048] fp16
